# 04: The 30-Day Incremental Study Habit System

**Source:** `youtube_pl.txt` (Lines 69-82)  

---

## Overview
A 4-week habit-stacking system that builds an unbreakable academic study routine by adding exactly **one new discipline each week**.

---

## Weekly Habit Progression

```
Week 1: Physical Environment & Time
└── 45 min desk session (phone in another room) + Physical paper goal
     │
Week 2: Spaced Review & Weekly Planning
└── + 10-min review of yesterday's material + 3 Sunday night priorities
     │
Week 3: The Feynman "Explain It Back"
└── + 2-min closed-book verbal explanation out loud
     │
Week 4: Active Retrieval Practice
└── + Practice exam questions 3x per week
```

### Week 1: Environment & Goal Anchor (1:10 - 2:11)
- **Protocol:** Study for 45 minutes daily.
- **Constraints:** Must sit at a desk (never in bed or couch) with the smartphone in another room.
- **Anchor:** Write the exact study goal on a physical piece of paper before beginning.

### Week 2: Spaced Review & Priority Setting (2:20 - 4:16)
- **Protocol:** Continue the 45-minute daily session.
- **Morning Habit:** Start with a 10-minute review of the previous day's notes.
- **Sunday Habit:** Every Sunday evening, write down the top 3 study priorities for the upcoming week.

### Week 3: Explain-It-Back / Feynman Technique (5:02 - 6:29)
- **Protocol:** Continue previous habits.
- **Session End:** Spend the final 2 minutes with closed notes, explaining the concept out loud as if teaching a peer. If you stumble, highlight that gap.

### Week 4: Active Retrieval Practice (6:35 - 8:07)
- **Protocol:** Complete practice questions three times a week.
- **Mechanics:** Forces active retrieval of memory rather than passive review, solidifying long-term synaptic retention.
