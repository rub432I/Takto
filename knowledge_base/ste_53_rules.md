# ASD-STE100 The 53 Grammar Constraints & 900-Word Dictionary Guide

**Reference:** `youtube_pl.txt` (Lines 41-58)  
**Historical Context:** ASD-STE100 (Simplified Technical English, est. 1986) was created for aircraft mechanics to eliminate ambiguity across international maintenance operations.

---

## The Core Principles

1. **One Word = One Meaning (Rule 1.11):** Each approved word is assigned exactly one definition and one grammatical category (verb, noun, or adjective).
2. **Active Voice Mandatory:** The subject performing the action must lead the sentence.
3. **Hard Sentence Caps (Rules 5.1 & 6.3):**
   - **Instructional Steps:** Maximum **20 words**.
   - **Descriptive Explanations:** Maximum **25 words**.
   - **Punctuation:** Semicolons (`;`) are strictly forbidden.

## The 53 Rules Summary Groups

```
Group 1: Words & Meanings (Rules 1.1 - 1.15)
├── Use approved words from the official dictionary.
├── Do not use technical nouns as verbs.
└── Keep terminology consistent across the entire document.

Group 2: Noun Clusters (Rules 2.1 - 2.5)
├── Do not string more than three nouns consecutively (e.g. "fuel pressure valve switch").
└── Clarify relationships using prepositions.

Group 3: Verbs & Tenses (Rules 3.1 - 3.8)
├── Use only simple present, simple past, and imperative tenses.
├── Ban the progressive form (-ing) when describing actions.
└── Eliminate auxiliary verbs and passive voice.

Group 4: Sentence Length & Structure (Rules 5.1 - 5.5)
├── Cap instructions at 20 words.
├── Cap descriptive sentences at 25 words.
└── Present only one instruction per sentence.
```
