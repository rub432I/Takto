## 📝 Transcript Content & Summary

Coding with AI is most effective when you treat the agent as a tactical worker while you remain the strategic leader (5:03). Instead of just asking the AI to 'code this,' follow a structured process to ensure quality and long-term health for your project:

Strategic Alignment: Before any code is written, use tools like Matt Pocock's "Grill with Docs" (12:35) to define a shared language and glossary. This ensures the AI understands your specific project context.
The Development Loop:
    1.  Define: Start with a clear technical specification (25:18).
    2.  Break Down: Divide the project into small, manageable tickets.
    3.  Build: Use sub-agents to implement features in parallel (34:35).
    4.  Review: Conduct adversarial code reviews focused on "merge danger" rather than line-by-line inspection (40:20).
    5.  Refine: Run a "retro" at the end to improve future performance (42:34).
Build Trust: Avoid manual line-by-line reviews by setting up strict typing, fast automated tests, and feedback loops (44:52). These act as a "straight jacket," preventing the AI from making mistakes.
Optimize Architecture: Focus on creating deep modules—large, self-contained sections of your codebase—which makes it much easier for AI to navigate and maintain your project (52:31).
