## 📝 Transcript Content & Summary

The aircraft standard mentioned is ASD-STE100, also known as Simplified Technical English (STE) (1:40). It was created in 1986 to ensure that maintenance manuals for aircraft were perfectly clear and easy to understand for mechanics around the world, regardless of their native language (4:07-4:29).

Key features of this standard include:

Restricted Vocabulary: It uses a dictionary of roughly 900 approved words, where each word is assigned exactly one meaning and one grammatical role to eliminate ambiguity (5:03-5:12).
Grammar Constraints: It includes 53 specific rules that enforce simple sentence structures, such as using active voice and banning complex verb constructions (1:46, 6:10).
Length Limits: There are hard caps on sentence length—usually 20 words for instructions and 25 words for descriptive sentences—to keep information digestible (6:35-6:40).
