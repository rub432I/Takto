## 📝 Transcript Content & Summary

The Cook skill is an automated workflow created by Leon Lin that executes his AI design process as a repeatable loop (12:53-13:05). Instead of performing every step manually, you use this tool to manage the entire design-to-review lifecycle for your app.

How the Cook skill works:

Setup and Configuration: After installing it via your terminal, you define your project parameters (13:05-13:25). When you run it, the tool asks questions about your app, references, desired quality, and resource usage limits (13:38-14:09).
The Loop: It operates in rounds. It starts by gathering design references, creating a design brief, and building the site (14:11-14:52).
Automated Feedback: Once a version is built, the skill triggers a separate judge—often a different AI model—to review the work against your specified quality bar (14:55-15:03).
Iterative Improvement: Based on the judge's score, the tool initiates subsequent rounds, refining the design until it successfully reaches your target score (15:03-15:08).

It maintains a dedicated `cook` folder within your project that stores all necessary files, including the design brief, historical screenshots, comparison side-by-sides, and logs of the grading process (14:21-14:44).
