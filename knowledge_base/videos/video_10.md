## 📝 Transcript Content & Summary

The 30-day plan incrementally builds a study routine by adding one new habit each week (0:36-0:44). The weekly breakdown is as follows:

Week 1: Establish the habit of studying for 45 minutes daily at a desk with your phone in another room. Before starting, write your study goal on a physical piece of paper (1:05-2:11).
Week 2: Incorporate a 10-minute review of the previous day's material at the start of every session. Additionally, write down your three study priorities for the week every Sunday night (2:20-4:16).
Week 3: At the end of every study session, close your notes and spend 2 minutes explaining the topic out loud as if teaching someone else (5:02-6:29).
Week 4: Add practice questions three times a week to actively force information retrieval from your memory (6:35-8:07).
