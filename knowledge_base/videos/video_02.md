## 📝 Transcript Content & Summary

The Karpathy method for building with AI, as presented in the video, is structured into three distinct layers to bridge the gap between human intent and AI execution:

1.  The Spec (0:28 - 3:33): This layer focuses on bridging your contextual understanding with AI's computational power. Instead of high-level planning, you must create a detailed specification by working with the AI. This involves uncovering your true goal (the decision or conclusion the project drives), using an agile approach by breaking tasks into smaller, manageable buckets, and being highly precise to minimize AI assumptions.

2.  The Verifier (3:34 - 8:47): Because AI often lacks context and can confidently provide incorrect answers, you need a robust verification system. This involves defining clear evaluation criteria before starting, using a second AI model as a critic to grade the output of the first, and pulling in external signals or data to confirm that the AI’s work is accurate and successful.

3.  The Environment (8:48 - 12:35): This layer establishes a permanent, optimized workshop for your AI agent. You should maintain a Claude.md file that gets automatically injected into prompts to define rules, build your own LLM knowledge base to manage project-specific data, create reusable custom skills for repeated tasks, and set rule-based guardrails at the tool level to ensure the AI follows strict boundaries.
