## 📝 Transcript Content & Summary

Building an effective AI specification (or "spec") is the critical first layer of the Karpathy method to ensure the AI truly understands your intent rather than making assumptions. According to the video, you can create a high-quality spec by following these three steps:

1.  Uncover your true goal: Instead of just giving the AI a task (e.g., "write a report"), define the underlying decision or conclusion you need that report to drive (1:48-2:09). You can prompt Claude by asking it: "Interview me to identify the goal of this project" to help extract the necessary information from you.

2.  Be agile with your specking: Avoid the "waterfall" method of dumping all instructions at once. Instead, adopt an agile approach by breaking your project into smaller, manageable buckets (2:13-2:48). Ask Claude to "bias towards smaller and more compartmentalized specs," review the output at each checkpoint, and iterate accordingly.

3.  Be precise to limit assumptions: Every assumption an AI makes creates an opportunity for it to drift away from your intended result (2:54-3:12). To use your own brain effectively, explicitly tell the AI: "Make me verify key decisions to ensure nothing is missed." Being highly precise ensures the AI builds exactly what you need.
