## 📝 Transcript Content & Summary

Andrej Karpathy utilizes AI as an essential assistant for high-level engineering, focusing on efficiency and delegated workflows rather than manual coding. His approach includes several key habits:

Tooling: He primarily uses Claude Code (the Mac desktop app) running Opus 5.5 on a "medium effort" setting, intentionally avoiding writing, reading, or executing code himself (0:22-0:43).
Prompting Strategy: He employs "aircraft repair manual" English—a style that is short, plain, and avoids unnecessary vocabulary to prevent the model from misinterpreting instructions (0:44-1:13).
Visual Logic: He prefers visual output, such as diagrams or HTML pages, because they allow him to process information significantly faster than reading long blocks of text (1:33-1:55).
Delegation Framework: Karpathy creates systems where Opus 5.5 orchestrates tasks, delegating smaller, specific actions to cheaper Sonnet sub-agents (3:28-3:55).
Knowledge Sharing: Rather than sharing app code (which he views as an abstraction), he shares the core data and the specific prompt used, empowering others to build their own custom implementations (3:56-4:34).
Human-AI Interaction: He is notably polite to his AI tools, often using formal greetings and pleasantries in his prompts, suggesting he views the human-AI relationship as a significant part of the creative process (4:35-5:07).
