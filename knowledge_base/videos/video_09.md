## 📝 Transcript Content & Summary

The 30-day plan is an incremental system designed to build a study routine by adding one new habit each week for four weeks (0:36-0:44). Here is the breakdown of the plan:

Week 1: Sit at a desk (not in bed or on a couch) to study for 45 minutes with your phone in another room. Plan the session on a physical piece of paper before you begin (1:10-2:11).
Week 2: In addition to the daily 45-minute session, perform a 10-minute review of the previous day's material at the start of your session. Also, write down your three study priorities for the week every Sunday night (2:20-4:16).
Week 3: Continue the previous habits and add a 2-minute "explain it back" session at the end of every study block. Close your notes and verbally explain the topic as if teaching a friend to ensure you truly understand it (5:02-6:29).
Week 4: Add a final layer by completing practice questions three times a week to force retrieval of information from memory, which helps solidify learning (6:35-8:07).
