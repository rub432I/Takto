## 📝 Transcript Content & Summary

According to the video, achieving high-quality AI-generated designs relies on a structured process rather than long prompts. Here are the key steps to improve your AI design workflow:

1.  Use Strong Visual References: Instead of lengthy descriptions, provide the AI with 9–12 high-quality reference images from platforms like Dribbble or Awwwards (0:53-1:36). This helps the AI understand layout, spacing, and typography.
2.  Design Mockups First: Build quick, simple web mockups before full development (4:09-4:34). This allows you to iterate on styles without consuming excessive tokens.
3.  Set a Numerical Quality Bar: Give the AI a specific goal (e.g., 99% accuracy) (5:03-6:32). This forces the AI to keep working beyond the first functional version.
4.  Ban Design 'Slop': Maintain a list of forbidden patterns—like generic fonts or overused background colors—to prevent common "AI-made" appearances (8:48-10:14).
5.  Build Iteratively: Separate your process. Focus on the static design first, and only add animations afterward using reference videos to guide motion (10:15-11:11).
6.  Use Independent Evaluation: Let a separate AI model or agent judge your design, as the original builder may be biased (11:53-12:53).

For those looking to automate this, the Cook skill can be installed to loop these steps from design through to review (12:53-15:08).
